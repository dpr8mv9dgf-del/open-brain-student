Capture a YouTube video

To get the transcript:
  1. Click Open video & transcript below (opens YouTube in a new tab).
  2. Under the video, click ...more, then Show transcript.
  3. Select all the transcript text, copy it, and paste it below.

Capture a web page

For security, browsers won't let this app read other websites directly, so you paste the text yourself:
  1. Paste the link below and click Open page.
  2. On that page, select all (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A) and copy (Ctrl+C or Cmd+C).
  3. Come back here and paste it into the big box.
